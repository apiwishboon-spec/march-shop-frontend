The Beginning

ART&INK started with a simple idea: make art wearable and accessible for everyone.

🇹🇭

Born in Thailand

The project was founded in Thailand by Apiwish Anutaravanichkul (Boon) together with a small team of friends and mentors from the school planetarium community. What began as a small experiment quickly turned into a mission to bring original artwork to everyday clothing.

🎭

Art by Real Artists

Every design is created by a real artist and printed by hand using screen printing. We focus on turning artwork into something you can wear — not mass-produced graphics, but pieces made with care, attention, and creative intention.

👥

For Students & Creators

ART&INK is built for students and young creators who want something different. Our goal is simple: cool designs, good quality, and prices that people can actually afford.

🌱

Small & Growing

We are a small Thailand-based brand, and we're just getting started. Each order is printed as it comes in, allowing us to focus on quality instead of overproduction.

✨

More Than Clothing

ART&INK is more than a clothing shop. It's a growing space where art, creativity, and everyday life come together.
