Our Story

Where Art Meets Everyday Life

Born in Thailand, ART&INK brings original artwork to clothing you can wear every day.

🇹🇭

Born in Thailand

Founded in Thailand by Apiwish Anutaravanichkul (Boon) together with a small team of friends and mentors from the school planetarium community. What began as a small experiment quickly turned into a mission to bring original artwork to everyday clothing.

🎭

Art by Real Artists

Every design is created by a real artist and printed by hand using screen printing. We focus on turning artwork into something you can wear — made with care, attention, and creative intention.

👥

For Students & Creators

ART&INK is built for students and young creators who want something different. Cool designs, good quality, and prices that people can actually afford.

🌱

Small & Growing

We are a small Thailand-based brand, just getting started. Each order is printed as it comes in — quality over overproduction, always.

✨

More Than Clothing

ART&INK is more than a clothing shop. It's a growing space where art, creativity, and everyday life come together — one shirt at a time.
