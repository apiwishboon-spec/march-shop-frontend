Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ART&INK products and services

👕 Products & Quality

What materials are your T-shirts made of? ▼

Our T-shirts are made from 100% premium cotton with the following specifications:

  • 180-200 GSM fabric weight for durability
  • Pre-shrunk to minimize size changes
  • Reactive dye for long-lasting color
  • Soft, breathable finish
  • Double-stitched seams for durability

All our shirts are screen-printed using high-quality, eco-friendly inks that are safe for skin and environment.

How do I choose the right size? ▼

We offer sizes S, M, L, and XL. Here's our size guide:

  • S: Chest 36-38", Length 26-27" (Fits XS-S)
  • M: Chest 39-41", Length 28-29" (Fits M)
  • L: Chest 42-44", Length 30-31" (Fits L)
  • XL: Chest 45-48", Length 32-33" (Fits XL)

Our shirts have a modern fit - not too tight, not too loose. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more relaxed fit.

How should I wash and care for my ART&INK T-shirt? ▼

To keep your ART&INK T-shirt looking great:

  1. Wash inside out with cold water
  2. Use gentle cycle and mild detergent
  3. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners
  4. Tumble dry low or hang to dry
  5. Do not iron directly on the print
  6. Do not dry clean

Following these care instructions will help maintain the print quality and fabric integrity for years.

🛒 Ordering Process

How do I place an order? ▼

Placing an order is simple:

  1. Browse our collection on the homepage
  2. Click "Order Now" on your desired design
  3. Select your size and quantity
  4. Scan the PromptPay QR code to pay
  5. Upload your payment slip
  6. Fill in your contact information
  7. Submit your order

You'll receive a confirmation email once we verify your payment.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it? ▼

Since our shirts are made to order, modifications are limited:

  • Within 1 hour: Contact us immediately - we may be able to modify
  • After 1 hour: Orders enter production and cannot be modified
  • Cancellations: Only possible before production starts

Please double-check your order details before submitting. If you need to make changes, contact us as soon as possible.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale orders? ▼

Yes! We offer bulk pricing for orders of 10+ shirts:

  • 10-24 shirts: 10% discount
  • 25-49 shirts: 15% discount
  • 50+ shirts: 20% discount + free shipping

For bulk orders, please contact us directly with your requirements. We can also discuss custom designs and special packaging.

💳 Payment Methods

What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We currently accept PromptPay only, which is Thailand's most popular payment method:

  • Mobile banking apps (KTB, SCB, BBL, etc.)
  • TrueMoney Wallet
  • GrabPay
  • Rabbit LINE Pay

Simply scan the QR code with your preferred banking app to complete payment.

Is it safe to pay with PromptPay? ▼

Yes, PromptPay is extremely safe:

  • Bank-level security encryption
  • No need to share card details
  • Transaction limits protect against fraud
  • Instant confirmation
  • Official Bank of Thailand payment system

We never store your payment information - everything goes through secure banking channels.

What happens after I make a payment? ▼

After payment:

  1. Take a screenshot of your payment confirmation
  2. Upload the payment slip on our order page
  3. Fill in your contact details
  4. Submit your order
  5. We'll verify your payment within 24 hours
  6. Production starts after verification

You'll receive email updates at each step of the process.

📦 Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take? ▼

Production and delivery times:

  • Production: 3-5 business days (made to order)
  • Bangkok Metro: 1-2 business days
  • Provincial Cities: 2-3 business days
  • Remote Areas: 3-5 business days

Total delivery time is typically 4-10 business days from order placement.

How much does shipping cost? ▼

Shipping rates within Thailand:

  • Bangkok Metro: ฿50
  • Provincial Cities: ฿70
  • Remote Areas: ฿90
  • Free Shipping: On orders over ฿500

    • International shipping is not currently available.

    Can I track my order? ▼

    Yes! Order tracking is available:

    • You'll receive a tracking number via email
    • Track on Thailand Post website
    • Real-time delivery updates
    • SMS notifications for major updates

    Tracking is typically available within 24 hours after shipment.

    🔄 Returns & Exchanges

    What is your return policy? ▼

    Our return policy:

    • 30-day return window from delivery date
    • Full refund for defective items
    • Store credit for wrong size/color (if our error)
    • No returns on custom orders

    Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached.

    How do I initiate a return or exchange? ▼

    To return or exchange:

    1. Contact us within 30 days of delivery
    2. Provide your order number and reason for return
    3. We'll send you a return shipping label
    4. Pack the item securely in original packaging
    5. Drop off at any Thailand Post location
    6. Refund/exchange processed within 5 business days

    Return shipping is free for defective items. For other returns, shipping costs may apply.

    What if my item arrives damaged? ▼

    If your item arrives damaged:

    1. Contact us immediately (within 48 hours)
    2. Send photos of the damage
    3. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund
    4. No return shipping required for damaged items

    We stand behind our quality and will make it right if there's any issue with your order.

    We're here to help! Contact our friendly support team.
