Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ART&INK products and services

🔍

👕 Product Questions

What material are the T-shirts made of? ▼
Our T-shirts are made from 100% premium cotton with a comfortable weight of 180gsm. They're soft, breathable, and perfect for everyday wear. The fabric is pre-shrunk to maintain its shape after washing.
How do I choose the right size? ▼
We recommend checking our size guide on the product page. Our T-shirts follow standard Asian sizing. If you're between sizes, we suggest sizing up for a more comfortable fit. Model measurements are available for reference.
How do I wash my ART&INK T-shirt? ▼
To keep your ART&INK T-shirt looking great: Wash inside out in cold water (30°C), use mild detergent, avoid bleach, tumble dry on low heat or hang dry, iron inside out if needed. This preserves the print quality and fabric.
Are the designs screen-printed? ▼
Yes! All our designs are professionally screen-printed using high-quality, eco-friendly inks. This ensures vibrant colors, durability, and a premium finish that won't crack or fade easily with proper care.
Do you offer custom designs? ▼
We do offer custom design services for bulk orders (10+ pieces). Please contact us with your design ideas and requirements. We'll work with you to create unique ART&INK designs for your team, event, or business.

💳 Payment Questions

What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We currently accept PromptPay payments, which is Thailand's most popular payment method. Simply scan the QR code with your mobile banking app to complete payment securely and instantly.
How does PromptPay payment work? ▼
After placing your order, you'll see a PromptPay QR code. Open your mobile banking app (Bangkok Bank, Kasikorn, SCB, etc.), scan the QR code, enter the amount, and confirm payment. The payment goes directly to our account.
Is my payment information secure? ▼
Yes! PromptPay uses bank-level security encryption. We never store your banking information. The payment is processed directly between your bank and ours through the secure PromptPay network.
What if my payment fails? ▼
If your payment fails, please check your internet connection and ensure you have sufficient funds. Try again with a fresh QR code. If issues persist, contact your bank or reach out to our customer support for assistance.

📦 Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take? ▼
Standard delivery within Thailand takes 3-5 business days after payment confirmation. Express delivery (1-2 business days) is available for an additional fee. International shipping takes 7-14 business days.
Do you ship outside Thailand? ▼
Yes, we ship internationally! Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. International orders are shipped via Thailand Post with tracking. Contact us for specific shipping rates to your country.
How much does shipping cost? ▼
Standard shipping within Thailand is 50 THB. Express shipping is 100 THB. International shipping starts from 300 THB. Free standard shipping is available for orders over 1,000 THB within Thailand.
Can I track my order? ▼
Yes! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package on the Thailand Post website or your respective national postal service for international orders.

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy? ▼
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the item is unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging. Custom designs and sale items are final sale. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective.
How do I exchange for a different size? ▼
To exchange sizes, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. We'll arrange for the exchange once we receive the original item. Size exchanges are subject to availability. You'll only need to pay shipping costs for the exchange.
What if my item arrives damaged? ▼
If your item arrives damaged, please contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We'll arrange for a free replacement or full refund, including return shipping costs. Please inspect your item upon delivery.
Can I cancel my order? ▼
Orders can be cancelled before payment confirmation. Once payment is received and production begins, cancellations may not be possible as our T-shirts are made to order. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel.

📞 Contact & Support

How do I contact customer service? ▼
You can reach us through our contact form, email us at support@art-ink.com, or message us on social media. We typically respond within 24 hours during business days (Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM ICT).
What are your business hours? ▼
Our customer service team is available Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM ICT (Indochina Time). We also monitor messages on weekends and respond to urgent inquiries. Production and shipping operate on business days only.
Can I order in bulk? ▼
Yes! We offer bulk discounts for orders of 10+ pieces. Bulk orders may take 7-14 days for production. Contact us with your requirements for a custom quote and timeline. We also offer customization services for bulk orders.
Do you have a physical store? ▼
Currently, we operate online only to keep our prices affordable. This allows us to focus on quality and design rather than expensive retail overhead. However, we occasionally participate in pop-up events and markets in Bangkok.
