🎨 Product Care

Recommended Care Instructions

  • Machine wash cold (30°C or below)
  • Wash inside out to protect prints
  • Use mild detergent, no bleach
  • Tumble dry low or hang dry
  • Do not iron directly on prints
  • Avoid dry cleaning

Following these care instructions ensures the longevity of your artwork shirt and maintains print quality. Treat your Chromink shirt like the artwork it is - with care and attention to detail.
